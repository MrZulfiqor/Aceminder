          Cookies keep your key ready

          Aceminder stores your Gemini API key in a cookie on this device so your practice tests are ready every visit. The key is only ever sent to Gemini.

          Two-minute setup

          Connect your Gemini API key

          Aceminder asks Gemini to write and mark your questions. With your own key, every test runs on your own quota — no monthly ceiling and no waiting on anyone else.

          1. 1
            Open Google AI Studio

            aistudio.google.com/apikey Sign in with your Google account and choose Create API key.

          2. 2
            Copy the whole key

            It starts with AIza and is about 39 characters long. Copy all of it, with no spaces.

          3. 3
            Paste it here once

            Save it and Aceminder remembers it in this browser, so you never have to type it again on this device.

          Cookies are off, so the key is kept only until you close this tab.

          Your key stays in this browser and is sent only to this local server, which forwards it to Google's Gemini API. It is never written to a file or shared. Testing it uses no generation quota.
